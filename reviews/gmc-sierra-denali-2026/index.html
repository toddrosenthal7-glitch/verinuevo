Quick answers

Which has more torque, the Duramax or the 6.2 V8?

Per GMC/GM ratings, the 3.0L Duramax makes 495 lb-ft versus 460 lb-ft for the 6.2L V8; the V8 makes more horsepower (420 vs 305).

What transmission do both Sierra Denali engines use?

Both use a 10-speed automatic, per GMC.

Did VeriNuevo tow or road-test these trucks?

No. The video is a static walkaround; ratings are the manufacturer's.
