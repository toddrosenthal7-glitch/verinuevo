Who is behind VeriNuevo, and how are videos fact-checked?

VeriNuevo is the Verified Buyer Brief for new cars: honest, fact-checked walkarounds of new models, checked against the official spec sheet and focused on the features buyers often miss.

Expect short walkarounds, clear trim and option notes, and flags when something should be confirmed with the seller. Todd narrates every episode in his own voice.

Specs come from the manufacturer or cited sources linked in each video. We say clearly when something was not tested (no road tests, towing or fuel-economy claims unless stated).

Sponsors will never be automakers or dealers, and sponsored content will always be labeled.

VeriNuevo is an independent channel, not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by any automaker or dealer. Always confirm details with the seller before you buy.
