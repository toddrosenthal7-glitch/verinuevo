What do most people miss on the 2026 Buick Envision Sport Touring?

Watch on YouTube: 2026 Buick Envision Sport Touring: 5 Things Most People Miss

A walkaround of five details on the 2026 Buick Envision Sport Touring that shoppers often walk past, checked against Buick's published information.

Key specs and what buyers miss

  • Sport Touring adds 20-inch Carbon Flash wheels, gloss-black exterior accents, its own mesh grille, alloy sport pedals and a leather-wrapped flat-bottom steering wheel. Garnet is one of the ST interior colors.
  • Since the 2024 refresh, the gear selector is a stalk on the steering column, which frees up the console.
  • Every 2026 Envision gets a 30-inch-diagonal display, head-up display, wireless phone charging and a 9-speaker Bose system.
  • Standard all-wheel drive and a 2.0-liter turbo with 228 hp.
  • The filmed unit has a compact spare on a steel wheel under the cargo floor. AAA has found many new vehicles don't include a spare, so check on anything you're shopping.
